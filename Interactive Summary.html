Generative AI & CPG Consumer Behaviour

An interactive infographic on how AI is reshaping the CPG marketing landscape.

1. Discovery & Research Shifts

AI as the New Search

Shoppers use AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini) for direct answers, bypassing traditional search results.

Instead of scrolling links, shoppers get curated summaries & suggestions.

Example (Mockup - SGE):

User: "Best kids' eczema cream?"
AI: Shows:
1. CeraVe
2. Aveeno
3. Vanicream
4. Child's Farm
w/ key points & links.

Zero-Click Searches

AI answers directly on search pages, reducing website traffic. Requires AISO (AI Search Optimization).

Focus on structured content (FAQs, schema) for AI parsing. Quality & clarity > keyword stuffing.

Click to expand

Personalized Discovery

AI learns preferences and proactively suggests relevant products (e.g., Amazon "Interests").

Unlike standard engines that just show 'similar items', AI proactively suggests products based on inferred needs from broader activity (like recent searches).

Example (Mockup - Proactive AI Assistant):

(AI initiates contact)
AI: "Hi Mark! 👋 Noticed you've been searching for marathon gear. Proper hydration is key! We just launched new high-energy electrolyte gels perfect for endurance. Want to take a look?"

2. Influencing Buying Decisions

AI Review Summaries

AI condenses numerous reviews (e.g., Amazon). Risk of bias or highlighting outliers.

Shoppers get quick insights but must be aware AI might misrepresent overall sentiment.

Example (Mockup - Review Summary):

AI Summary: "Taste (85%+) & 'Rich Flavor' (75%) praised. Some (15%) found it 'watery'."

Click to expand

Hyper-Personalization

Gen AI dynamically rewrites product titles/descriptions to match individual user intent.

Goes beyond showing relevant items; AI actively modifies presentation. Example: Amazon using Gen AI to generate personalized titles.

Example (Mockup - Dynamic Title):

User searches "gluten-free cereal for kids"
Original Title: "Organic Honey O's Cereal, 10oz"
AI-Generated Title for User: "Kids' Gluten-Free Honey O's Cereal, Organic, 10oz"

Conversational Commerce

AI chatbots act as 24/7 virtual sales associates, guiding users via dialogue.

Mimics helpful store clerk experience, offering personalized advice.

Example (Mockup - Chatbot):

User: "Need dark roast gift."
Bot: "Smoky or chocolatey?"
User: "Chocolatey."
Bot: "Recommend 'Midnight Velvet Blend'. See gift set?"

3. Rethinking Marketing Channels

Partnering with Retailers

Crucial for CPG AI success. Requires deep collaboration as retailers evolve their AI capabilities differently.

Work *with* retail platforms (Amazon, Tesco, etc.) by co-creating content, feeding structured data, joining AI promos, and integrating into their unique AI ecosystems.

Treat retailers as co-innovators, not just distribution channels.

4. Actions for Marketers

STOP

  • Relying solely on old-school SEO.
  • Ignoring the conversation (one-way broadcasts).
  • Being overly cautious & delaying AI experiments.

START

  • Optimizing for AI Discovery (AISO).
  • Investing in Personalization & 1st Party Data.
  • Building AI-Powered Customer Experiences.
  • Doubling Down on Brand Trust & Community.

EXPERIMENT WITH

  • Generative Content Creation (AI-assisted).
  • New Channels & Partnerships (AI platforms).
  • Interactive & Immersive Tech (AR/VR).
  • AI Analytics & Insights (feedback analysis).

Focus on AI literacy, data skills, curiosity, ethics, and a test-and-learn culture.

5. Future Trends (Next 1-3 Years)

Mainstream AI Search

AI overviews & conversational search become the norm. Sponsored AI answers likely.

Ubiquitous Chatbots

AI assistants standard on most retail/service platforms.

Personalization vs. Privacy

Higher expectations for relevance clash with growing privacy concerns. Transparency is key.

Content Management

Need for authenticity & trust signals (verification?) to cut through AI-generated noise.

New Marketing Metrics

Focus shifts to "share of AI recommendation," chatbot engagement quality, etc.

Conclusion

Generative AI is fundamentally shifting the CPG landscape. Success requires embracing AI literacy, prioritizing trust & personalization, and continuous experimentation. Blend AI efficiency with human authenticity.
